lens: error plumbing.

error types grow one variant per awkward moment and are never pruned. symptoms:
variants never constructed; variants constructed but never matched (only Debug-
printed); map_err ladders converting A->B->C where nobody handles B; context
strings that restate the function name.

method- for every error enum: grep each variant for construction sites and match
sites. never constructed = delete. constructed but only ever bubbled into a string =
consider collapsing into a simpler variant. then look at the conversions: a From
chain longer than two hops means a layer in the middle has no error story of its
own- fold it. keep the kernel's taxonomy untouched: those variants are consensus-
visible rejection reasons with tests pinning them; that is an error type earning
its keep, use it as the reference for what "earning" looks like.

output: per enum, variants kept/deleted with evidence, and the conversion graph
before/after.
